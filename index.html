PlotterSim v1 — a pen-plotter simulator by Zachary Shade. How it works: your image is reduced to a small palette, every colour region is traced into outline paths, then each region is filled with overlapping back-and-forth marker strokes (slight transparency, so overlaps band darker — just like a real marker). The virtual plotter lifts its pen between strokes and drives to the pen dock to swap colours. No servers, no uploads, no tracking.
